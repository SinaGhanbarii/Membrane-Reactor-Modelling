Schematic of a packed-bed Pd-membrane water-gas shift reactor in three views. Panels (a) and (b) are longitudinal sections of a tubular membrane inside a cylindrical shell. The annulus between shell and tube is filled with catalyst particles through which the retentate gas flows from z = 0 to z = L. Hydrogen crosses the membrane wall into the bore. In (a) the sweep gas flows in the same direction as the retentate (co-current); in (b) it flows the opposite way (counter-current). Panel (c) is a to-scale cross-section A-A: a shell of 25.4 mm inner diameter, a catalyst annulus 7.9 mm wide, a membrane tube of 9.525 mm outer diameter, and the permeate bore in the centre.
